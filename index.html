Your house, ready for your notes

  1. Look around. In 3D, drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom; the buttons at the bottom jump to set views. Walk inside puts you in the house at eye level: walk with the arrow keys or W A S D (on a phone, drag the left side of the screen) and drag to look around. Doors open as you reach them. Floor plan shows the rooms.
  2. Click anything: a wall, window, roof, cabinet, sink, light, room or door. You can also click an open spot on the lawn or plan.
  3. Write what you think and attach photos of houses or details you like. Each note pins to the spot you clicked.
  4. Press "I'm finished" when you're done. Everything saves as you go; there's nothing to send.
