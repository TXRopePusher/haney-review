  1. Look around. In the 3D exterior, drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom; the buttons at the bottom jump to set views. Switch to Floor plan to see the rooms.

  3. Write what you think and attach photos of houses or details you like. Each note pins to the spot you clicked.
  4. Press "I'm finished" when you're done. Everything saves as you go; there's nothing to send.
